A joke that lands

Made with care counts here too. A joke has fewer parts than a watch, and every one of them has to be in exactly the right place.

01 Why

It’s the cheapest well-made thing there is, and you can give it away without losing it.

02 What I look for

Economy. A setup that doesn’t announce itself, a turn you didn’t see coming, and not one word more than it needs.

03 What I’ve learned

Timing is a mechanism, like a watch’s escapement: the pause is doing most of the work.
